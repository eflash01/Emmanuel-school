Our Programs

From early learning through college preparation, every stage builds on the last.

Elementary School

Kindergarten – Grade 5

Young learners build a strong foundation in reading, writing, and math through hands-on activities and small-group instruction.

  • Phonics-based literacy program
  • Hands-on science and math exploration
  • Art, music, and physical education

Middle School

Grade 6 – Grade 8

Students deepen their academic skills while developing independence, collaboration, and critical thinking.

  • Core subjects taught by subject specialists
  • Introductory foreign language courses
  • Clubs, sports, and leadership opportunities

High School

Grade 9 – Grade 12

College-preparatory coursework paired with personalized guidance to help students plan their next steps.

  • Advanced Placement (AP) courses
  • One-on-one college counseling
  • Internship and career readiness programs

Enrichment & After-School

All Grades

Beyond the classroom, students explore new interests and build skills through clubs, arts, and athletics.

  • Music, drama, and visual arts
  • Competitive and recreational sports teams
  • STEM clubs and academic competitions
