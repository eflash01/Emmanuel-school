Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers.

What is the admission process?

Our admission process involves completing an online application, submitting required documents, and scheduling a visit or interview with our admissions team.

What grades/ages does Bright Future Academy serve?

We serve students from Kindergarten through Grade 12.

Do you offer scholarships or financial aid?

Yes, merit-based and need-based scholarships are available — ask admissions for criteria.
